Eradication

  • Remove persistence: services, scheduled tasks, run keys, WMI subscriptions, new admin accounts
  • Remove remote access tooling installed by the actor (AnyDesk, ScreenConnect, Cobalt Strike beacons)
  • Patch or decommission the initial access vector
  • Rotate all service account, domain admin, and backup system credentials
  • Hunt for additional implants across the estate using collected IOCs
  • Confirm no remaining actor access before beginning restoration

Recovery

  • Rebuild tier-0 systems first from known-clean media
  • Restore data from backups verified to pre-date the intrusion
  • Scan restored systems for IOCs before reconnecting to production
  • Bring business-critical services back in priority order agreed with leadership
  • Monitor for re-encryption attempts or returning C2 traffic for at least 30 days
  • Complete regulatory, law enforcement, and customer notifications as advised by counsel

Lessons Learned

  • Document the full attack chain against MITRE ATT&CK
  • Record recovery time per critical system versus stated RTO
  • Evaluate backup coverage and isolation gaps that the incident exposed
  • Review the ransom payment decision process and its timeliness
  • Assign owners for hardening actions (MFA gaps, exposed RDP, EDR coverage)
  • Update the ransomware playbook with what actually happened
